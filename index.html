BK Bread + Salad Order Calculator

pro1000 method. Bread + Salad together (buns + Eisberg/Tomaten/Zwiebeln).

pro1000 = (yesterdayUsed ÷ yesterdayUmsatz) × 1000
neededUnits = plannedUmsatz × pro1000
availableUnits = workingStock − TKstock + incomingUnits
toOrderUnits = max(0, neededUnits − availableUnits)
toOrderPacks = ceil(toOrderUnits ÷ normalPackSize)
